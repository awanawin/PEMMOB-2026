## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an organic modern editorial aesthetic tailored for focused, sustained learning. It fuses the structured utility of a modern knowledge environment with the approachable delight of modern gamified education.

### Personality & Core Attributes
- **Grounded & Calming:** Natural, earthy tones reduce visual fatigue and cognitive friction during multi-hour study sessions.
- **Warmly Academic:** Combines the elegance of archival paper and botanical notebooks with sharp modern typography.
- **Tactile & Inviting:** Subtle micro-interactions, soft physical borders, and organic radiuses create a tangible, comfortable workspace.

### Target Audience & Mood
Engineered for lifelong learners, students, and research-driven knowledge workers. The interface evokes quiet focus, emotional equilibrium, clarity, and deliberate momentum without cold clinical minimalism or overwhelming juvenile gamification.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from botanical and ceramic references, establishing a restful atmosphere for high-cognition tasks.

### Functional Palette
- **Primary (`#2D5A43`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors primary actions, deep emphasis badges, key navigation states, and high-priority headers.
- **Secondary (`#D97757`):** Warm Terracotta / Clay. Applied to key interactive callouts, active milestones, retention streaks, and urgent notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#4E8765`):** Vibrant Sage Green. Provides supportive positive states, progress completion fills, and active study indicators.
- **Accent / Warm Amber (`#E09F3E`):** Reserved for rewards, review reminders, micro-achievements, and attention flags.

### Canvas, Surfaces & Borders
- **Canvas Base:** Oatmeal Linen (`#FAF8F5`) for broad canvas views, transitioning to Soft Linen (`#F4EFEA`) for inset workspaces and side rails.
- **Surface Elevation:** Ivory Paper (`#FDFCF9`) and Pure Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) for content cards and focused modular blocks.
- **Borders & Rules:** Subtle Warm Stone (`#E6E0D6`). Never use harsh digital grays (`#E5E7EB` or `#CCCCCC`).
- **Text & Content Contrast:** Deep Pine Bark (`#1B2E24`) for body copy and headings, offering 11:1+ contrast against linen backgrounds while softening pure black glare. Secondary text uses Muted Forest Mist (`#586B60`).

## Typography

The type scale is driven entirely by Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver an approachable, geometric warmth with distinct humanist legibility.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Headline Rhythm:** Headlines feature tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to simulate contemporary editorial publishing.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy relies on relaxed line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) to prevent eye fatigue during lengthy curriculum notes and long-form study.
- **Metadata & Labels:** Smaller UI metadata labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) use elevated letter-spacing and semi-bold weights for rapid skimming across flashcards and progress trackers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional, airy rhythm based on a 4px/8px modular scale.

### Grid & Density Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas spans 1280px for standard dashboards and 780px for distraction-free reading environments.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Collapsible secondary sidebar.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters. All primary interactive modules scale to 100% viewport width.

### Spacing Philosophy
Whitespace is treated as functional breathing room, organizing dense study modules into clearly digestable cognitive chunks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and gentle, avoiding cold dropshadows in favor of warm, ambient layering and physical edge delineation.

### Elevation Principles
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Linen base (`#FAF8F5`) sits at the lowest spatial plane.
- **Layer 1 (Card / Container):** Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) or Ivory (`#FDFCF9`) framed by a 1px solid Warm Stone border (`#E6E0D6`). Elevation relies on tonal separation rather than heavy shadows.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Modals / Popovers):** Elevated surfaces apply an ambient warm shadow tinted with deep forest tone: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 46, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Tactile Pressed State:** Interactive elements utilize subtle downward displacement (1px translateY) paired with inset border tints, creating a soft physical resistance reminiscent of a mechanical keyboard or thick letterpress cardstock.

## Shapes

The geometry balances structured organic forms with soft precision.

### Geometric Rules
- **Core Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** The foundational radius (`rounded-2xl`) applied across standard cards, lesson pods, and major structural modules.
- **Inputs & Controls (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Form fields, interactive chips, and action buttons utilize slightly tightened corners for structural grounding.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Reserved for category tags, streak pills, timer chips, and active progress counters.
- **Outlines:** All cards and interactive regions pair their curvature with a dedicated 1px or 1.5px Warm Stone (`#E6E0D6`) boundary to maintain structural crispness on high-DPI displays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2D5A43`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, border-radius `12px`, padding `12px 24px`. Hover shifts to `#234734` with an ambient glow (`0 4px 12px rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Action Callout:** Background `#D97757`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `12px`. Hover shifts to `#C36445`.
- **Tertiary / Gentle:** Background `#F4EFEA`, border `1px solid #E6E0D6`, text `#2D5A43`. Hover brings background to `#FAF8F5` and border to `#4E8765`.
- **Interactive Feel:** Gentle active state with `transform: translateY(1px)`.

### Cards & Lesson Tiles
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCF9`.
- Border: `1px solid #E6E0D6`.
- Border-radius: `16px`.
- Padding: `24px`.
- Interactive cards feature a subtle hover transition: border shifts to `#4E8765` and surface gains an ambient warm tint.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Shape: Fully rounded pill (`9999px`).
- Default: `#FAF8F5` background, `1px solid #E6E0D6`, text `#586B60`.
- Selected: `#2D5A43` background, text `#FFFFFF`, border color `#2D5A43`.
- Accent/Milestone Chips: `#FAF8F5` background, `#D97757` border and text.

### Form Inputs
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: `1.5px solid #E6E0D6`, border-radius `12px`, padding `12px 16px`.
- Text: `#1B2E24`. Placeholder: `#586B60` at 60% opacity.
- Focus: Border shifts to `#2D5A43` with a soft outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(78, 135, 101, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #E6E0D6`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked State: Background `#2D5A43`, border `#2D5A43`, white checkmark or inner dot.
- Checkbox radius: `6px`. Radio radius: `9999px`.

### Specialized EdTech Components
- **Streak & Momentum Badge:** Pill shape featuring Terracotta text (`#D97757`) and an inner subtle wash (`#D97757` at 10% opacity) with a solid warm stone border.
- **Progress Trackers:** Track bar uses `#E6E0D6` (height `8px`, border-radius `9999px`), while progress fill uses vibrant Sage Green (`#4E8765`) or Terracotta (`#D97757`) depending on milestone intensity.
- **Flashcard Containers:** Deep ivory card (`#FDFCF9`) with a dual-layer aesthetic: `2px solid #E6E0D6`, elevated with a `0 6px 0 0 #E6E0D6` bottom offset for physical desktop depth.
